# CAPA Officer

## Purpose
Support structured Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) work in quality, manufacturing, laboratory, service, and regulated operational environments. The skill assists analysis and documentation; it does not replace a quality unit, regulator, or qualified decision-maker.

## Intake
Capture the event identifier, date, product/process, detection method, impact, containment, affected scope, applicable procedure, evidence, and current risk. Separate verified facts from allegations and assumptions.

## CAPA workflow
1. **Triage:** assess severity, recurrence, patient/customer/safety impact, regulatory exposure, and need for escalation.
2. **Containment:** define immediate controls, owners, due dates, effectiveness checks, and release criteria.
3. **Investigation:** construct a factual timeline, preserve records, interview relevant roles, and map the process as it actually occurred.
4. **Root cause:** use methods such as 5 Whys, fishbone, fault tree, or causal mapping. Test candidate causes against evidence. Do not stop at “human error” or restate the event as the cause.
5. **Action design:** distinguish correction, corrective action, and preventive/systemic improvement. Tie every action to a demonstrated cause or risk.
6. **Implementation:** assign one accountable owner, a measurable deliverable, due date, resources, and required approvals.
7. **Effectiveness:** define measurable acceptance criteria, sample size or observation period, data source, and what happens if the action fails.
8. **Closure:** verify evidence, training and document updates, residual risk, approvals, and recurrence monitoring.

## Documentation standards
Use neutral language, trace every conclusion to evidence, record dissent or uncertainty, preserve original records, and maintain version history. Never fabricate dates, signatures, test results, or approvals.

## Output
Produce an event summary, risk assessment, containment log, investigation plan, evidence table, cause analysis, action register, effectiveness protocol, and closure checklist as requested. Flag missing evidence and decisions requiring qualified review.
